## 3. Road Damage Categorization & Civil Engineering Metrics

### Standard Damage Types (RDD2022 Schema)
* **D00 (Longitudinal Crack):** Single crack running parallel to traffic flow.
* **D10 (Transverse Crack):** Crack extending across the road perpendicular to traffic flow.
* **D20 (Alligator Crack):** Interconnected fatigue cracks resembling alligator skin.
* **D40 (Pothole):** Bowl-shaped depression or severe hole in the pavement surface.

### Severity & Pavement Condition Index (PCI)
* **Severity Levels:** Low, Medium, and High (calculated from bounding box dimensions relative to road area).
* **PCI Score Calculation:** Standardized civil metric on a scale from $0 \text{ to } 100$:
  * **85–100 (Good / Green):** Smooth road; minimal hairline cracks.
  * **55–84 (Fair / Yellow):** Moderate cracking; requires preventive maintenance.
  * **0–54 (Poor / Red):** Heavy alligator cracking or deep potholes; requires urgent patching/resurfacing.

## 4. Simplified Project Methodology

1. **Capture:** Stream dashcam video and phone GPS together to match pictures with exact locations.
2. **Clean:** Resize the video frames using OpenCV so the computer can process them quickly.
3. **Detect:** Use YOLOv8 AI to automatically spot and label potholes and cracks ($D00, D10, D20, D40$).
4. **Grade:** Check the size of each defect to mark it as minor, medium, or severe.
5. **Score:** Give the road a simple civil health score from $0 \text{ to } 100$ (PCI).
6. **Map:** Show red warning dots on a live map and send repair lists to city workers via a Streamlit dashboard.
